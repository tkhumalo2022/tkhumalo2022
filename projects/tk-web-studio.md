## The problem

A small business often needs more than a good-looking website. Customers need to understand the offer and know what to do next. The owner needs enough information to respond without chasing the same missing details over WhatsApp.

I started TK Web Studio to work on both sides of that problem: **make the public website clear, then make the enquiry easier to handle**.

## What I've built

### 1. Public website

I designed and developed a mobile-first studio website with clear services, pricing, selected work, interactive concept demos and direct enquiry paths.

- [See the website](https://tkwebstudio.company)
- [View the work](https://tkwebstudio.company/work)

### 2. Enquiry and quotation journey

Rather than asking a visitor to write a perfect brief, the public contact form asks for essential contact details and a short description first. Budget, timing and other project details can be supplied when useful.

The **private** application code also covers business workflows for tracking leads, quotations, projects and payment records. I don't publish admin screenshots, customer records or private source code.

### 3. Practical AI and automation work

I am building enquiries, lead organisation, human handover and other workflow modules around specific business problems. The aim is to collect useful context and leave pricing, approvals and sensitive decisions with a person.

These are **project-specific capabilities under development**, not a claim that every advertised integration is already deployed for customers.

### 4. TK Video Studio (ongoing)

A separate development workstream explores short-form video production: prompt/storyboard planning, editable scenes, vertical previews and FFmpeg-based MP4 rendering.

The source is currently part of the private TK Web Studio project. It is **not presented here as a public SaaS launch, a public GitHub repository or proof of paying users**.

## Design and engineering decisions

- **Write for customers.** The first screen should explain the offer and next action.
- **Prioritise phones.** Many prospective customers arrive through WhatsApp and social links.
- **Build trust through proof.** Separate client work, concept demos, internal features and plans.
- **Keep automation accountable.** Use clear human handovers for decisions that need approval.
- **Protect business data.** Keep credentials, customer records and operational source private.
